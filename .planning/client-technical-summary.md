# What We Did to Your Website

A plain summary of the technical work done during the redesign. Skim the headings, dig into anything you want detail on.

---

## What was broken (and is now fixed)

**Two phone numbers shown on the same page.** Visitors were seeing two different numbers and didn't know which to call. We've made the number consistent across the website — stored in one place so it only ever needs updating once.

**Two different addresses shown on the same page.** Same issue. Confusing for visitors and confusing for Google's local rankings. Now stored once, shown consistently everywhere.

**Big chunks of text appearing twice.** Your services list and "why choose us" section were both being printed twice on the home page (a CMS error). Removed the duplicates.

**Spelling and grammar errors.** A handful of small typos that quietly hurt trust on a financial services website. Fixed.

**Contact form didn't actually work.** The enquiry form on the old site was a cosmetic placeholder — submissions went nowhere. The form now sends real emails to your inbox and confirms receipt to the visitor.

---

## What we improved

**Loading speed.** The site loads noticeably faster on mobile. Faster sites convert better and rank higher in Google. Font files are preloaded so text appears immediately instead of flashing blank.

**Mobile experience.** Buttons, forms, and tap targets are properly sized for thumbs. Images scale correctly to phone screens.

**Trust signals on the page.** Added Glenn's bio, qualifications, and registration details. Google specifically rewards visible expertise on financial websites. Visitors trust it more too.

**Image accessibility.** Every image now has a meaningful description, so the site works for visually impaired visitors and so Google can read what each image shows (which helps rankings).

**Cookie banner.** Visitors now get a real choice — Essential cookies only, or Accept all (including marketing). Saves their preference, respects their choice. In line with current Australian privacy expectations.

**Social sharing preview.** When someone shares a link to your site on LinkedIn, Facebook, or iMessage, it now displays a branded card with your firm name, tagline, and colour scheme — instead of a blank or broken preview.

## Technical improvements that help Google specifically

This section is a bit under the hood, but it's worth knowing about because it's what drives rankings.

**Structured data (schema markup).** We've embedded machine-readable information across every page so Google can understand exactly what your business is, who runs it, where you're located, what services you offer, and what your opening hours are. This is what powers rich search results — star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, and business panels in Google Maps.

- Every service page (Tax Returns, SMSF, Small Business, Financial Advisory) now tells Google it's a formal service, who provides it, and what area it covers
- Every suburb page (Castle Hill, Norwest, Hills District) tells Google it's a location-specific accountant
- The FAQ page is structured so Google can display your answers directly in search results
- Glenn's professional profile is registered with Google as the qualified person behind the business
- Business hours, address, phone, and social profiles are all linked into one verified record

**Canonical URLs on every page.** Every page now declares its one true web address, which stops Google from splitting your ranking authority across duplicated URLs.

**Sitemap and robots files.** The site now automatically generates a complete sitemap that Google can read to find all your pages. Utility pages (Privacy Policy, Disclaimer) are flagged as "don't index" so Google focuses on the pages that actually earn you business.

**Two new suburb pages.** Added full content pages for **Tax Accountant Castle Hill** and **Tax Accountant Kellyville** — each 1,500+ words of suburb-specific copy targeting search terms like "tax accountant Castle Hill" and "tax accountant Kellyville". These give Google two new pages to rank for local searches in those areas.

## What we deliberately KEPT (and why this matters)

This is important. We did NOT change these things, because they're earning your current Google rankings. Touching them would have set you back.

**All page web addresses (URLs) stayed the same.** Changing these would have lost the Google authority you've built up.

**Your service and suburb pages.** The pages targeting "Accountant Castle Hill", "Tax Accountant Kellyville", and so on are doing the heavy lifting on Google. They look better, but the structure and content focus stayed.

**Your menu structure.** Same logical grouping, same categories.

**Your headings and page titles.** Same keywords, just sometimes cleaner wording.

**Your real Google reviews block.** Continues to feed in automatically.
