    Common Questions About Our Accounting Services

    How much do your accounting services cost?

    Our fees depend on what you need, but we’re always upfront about pricing. Individual tax returns start from competitive rates, and business accounting packages are customised to your requirements. We’ll give you a clear fixed-fee quote before starting any work – no hidden surprises.

    What accounting software do you work with?

    We work with all the major platforms, including Xero, MYOB, Easy business App and QuickBooks Online. We can help you migrate to cloud accounting and assist you with the transition.

    Do I have to come to your office or can we meet online?

    We offer face-to-face meetings at our Baulkham Hills office or virtual consultations via Zoom, Teams or phone. We’re flexible because we know everyone’s busy.

    When should I get in touch with an accountant?

    Most people think of accountants only at tax time, but honestly, the best time to engage an accountant is year-round. Proactive planning throughout the year delivers way better results than scrambling at the last minute.

    What do I need for my tax return?

    We’ll send you a comprehensive checklist, but generally you’ll need: payment summaries, receipts for expenses, investment statements, rental property records, and any previous tax returns.

    Do you only work with businesses in Baulkham Hills?

    While we’re based in Baulkham Hills, we work with clients across theHills District, South West Sydney, Greater Sydney, and even Australia-wide through our online services. Location isn’t a barrier.

    Can you help with crypto taxes?

    Absolutely! Crypto tax can be complicated, but we know how to handle it properly and make sure you’re compliant with ATO requirements.

    What if I haven't lodged tax returns for a few years?

    No judgment here – it happens! We can help you get caught up and back on track with the ATO. The sooner you sort it out, the better, so give us a call and we’ll work through it together.
